## What it checks

- **Video format.** AVC, HEVC, VC-1 and MPEG Video come from discs. Anything else is a red flag.
- **Re-encode.** An x264 or x265 settings line means someone re-encoded the file.
- **Video bitrate.** Below 15 Mb/s for 1080p or 30 Mb/s for UHD is low for a disc. This is a rule of thumb.
- **Width.** A remux keeps the full frame, so the width should be 1920 or 3840.
- **HDR.** A 2160p file should have an HDR format line.
- **Lossless audio.** At least one TrueHD, DTS-HD MA, PCM or FLAC track.
- **Languages.** Every audio and text track needs a language.
- **PCM audio.** Convert PCM to FLAC to save space.
- **Chapters.** The Menu section should be present.

These are the same checks as [Part 2 of Finding a good remux](/remux/finding-a-good-remux/#part-2-check-the-remux-file), run for you.
