## What it reads

<details class="ui-reference">
<summary>What it reads (reference)</summary>

<DefinitionTable
  label="What it reads"
  columns={['MediaInfo line', 'Becomes']}
  rows={[
    ['Transfer characteristics: PQ', '<code>--transfer smpte2084</code> and the HDR10 flags'],
    ['Mastering display color primaries', 'The colour part of <code>--master-display</code>'],
    ['Mastering display luminance', 'The <code>L(max,min)</code> part of <code>--master-display</code>'],
    ['Maximum Content Light Level, Maximum Frame-Average Light Level', '<code>--max-cll "MaxCLL,MaxFALL"</code>'],
    ['Chroma subsampling: 4:2:0 (Type 2)', '<code>--chromaloc 2</code>'],
    ['Color range', '<code>--range</code>'],
  ]}
/>

</details>

x265 wants the colour coordinates in steps of 0.00002 and the brightness in steps of 0.0001 cd/m². So a green x of 0.265 becomes 13250, and 1000 cd/m² becomes 10000000.
