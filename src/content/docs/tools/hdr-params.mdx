## What it reads

| MediaInfo line | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| Transfer characteristics: PQ | `--transfer smpte2084` and the HDR10 flags |
| Mastering display color primaries | The colour part of `--master-display` |
| Mastering display luminance | The `L(max,min)` part of `--master-display` |
| Maximum Content Light Level, Maximum Frame-Average Light Level | `--max-cll "MaxCLL,MaxFALL"` |
| Chroma subsampling: 4:2:0 (Type 2) | `--chromaloc 2` |
| Color range | `--range` |

x265 wants the colour coordinates in steps of 0.00002 and the brightness in steps of 0.0001 cd/m². So a green x of 0.265 becomes 13250, and 1000 cd/m² becomes 10000000.
