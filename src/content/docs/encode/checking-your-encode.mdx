## Who this is for

Anyone who has finished an x264 or x265 encode.

## When you use this

Every time, before the upload. It takes ten minutes and saves a rejected upload.

## Before you start

- The finished encode
- The source it came from
- MediaInfo

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Check the settings.** Copy the MediaInfo text of the encode into the [header checker](/tools/header-checker/). It must show CRF or 2-pass and no problems.

   | Encoder | CRF shows | 2-pass shows |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | x264 | `rc=crf` and `crf=` | `rc=2pass` and `bitrate=` |
   | x265 | `rc=crf` and `crf=` | `rc=abr`, `bitrate=` and `stats-read=` above 0 |

   x265 never writes `2pass`. An `rc=abr` line with `stats-read=0` is a 1-pass encode, which most trackers reject.

2. **Check the video lines.** In MediaInfo: resolution is the cropped size, frame rate matches the source, bit depth is 8 (x264) or 10 (x265). For HDR, the mastering display values match the source.

3. **Check the duration.** It must match the source to the second. A shorter file means the encode stopped early.

4. **Watch the start, the middle and the end.** Look for green frames, blocks, or audio drifting from the lips.

5. **Compare frames with the source.** Take screenshots of the same frames from source and encode, and compare them, for example on [slow.pics](https://slow.pics/). Look at faces, grain and dark scenes.

6. **Check the name against the tracks.** Resolution, source, audio codec, channels and encoder in the name must match MediaInfo exactly.

</Steps>

<Aside title="Screenshots for the upload">
  Most trackers ask for screenshots at the release's own resolution. Upload Assistant takes them for you. Pick frames with faces and detail, not black screens or credits.
</Aside>

## Stop and ask someone if

- The header checker says 1-pass ABR, CBR or first pass and you did not choose that.
- The duration is off by more than a second.
- The encode looks clearly worse than the source in normal playback.

## What good looks like

The header checker is all green, durations match, playback is clean, and the name describes the file exactly.
