The sizes are typical ranges, not rules. Long films, grainy films and animation all move them.

## What you can make from what

| You have | You can make |
| --- | --- |
| Full disc | Remux, encode |
| Remux | Encode |
| WEB-DL | Nothing new. Upload it as it is, or not at all. |
| Someone else's encode | Nothing. An encode made from an encode is rejected everywhere. |

<Aside title="Why the source decides everything">
  A 1080p encode from a UHD disc usually looks better than one from the 1080p Blu-ray, because the UHD often has a newer master. That is also why some trackers only allow x265 encodes from UHD sources.
</Aside>

## Editions and masters

The same film can have several Blu-rays from different labels, countries or years. They can differ in:

- **Master**: a 2020 4K restoration and a 2008 transfer can look like different films.
- **Cut**: theatrical, director's cut, extended.
- **Audio**: original mono mix or a new 5.1 remix.
- **Region**: some editions add or remove scenes, subtitles or audio tracks.

[Finding a good remux](/remux/finding-a-good-remux/) shows how to compare editions before you pick one.
